Why Choose Us

DiDi Pizza - Fresh Pizza Daily in the Heart

Are you looking for a great stop in the heart of Hoi An to enjoy true, fresh pizza every day? Do not miss DiDi Pizza, an impressive pizza sanctuary bringing a memorable wood-fired experience.

Fresh Daily Pizza

Thin, crispy dough fermented slowly, topped with carefully selected, fresh local ingredients.

Unique Delicate Flavor

Harmonious blend of rich mozzarella, San Marzano tomato sauce, and distinct chef seasonings.

Cozy Hoi An Space

Clean, romantic atmosphere with charming street views to soak in the tranquil Hoi An lifestyle.

Dedicated Service

Warm hospitality, fast serving times, and great value for an authentic Italian culinary journey.

Interactive Dough-Tossing Show

Watch Chef DiDi perform bakery techniques and throw pizza dough live right in front of your eyes before placing it into the 400°C stone oven!
